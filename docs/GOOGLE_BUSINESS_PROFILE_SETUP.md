## 1. Executive Summary & Google Guidelines for Individual Practitioners

Google Business Profile allows licensed legal practitioners (attorneys / advocates) to maintain an individual practitioner listing under their real-world name.

### Crucial Naming Rule
- **Approved Business Name:** `Dr. Surajit Sinha`
- **Prohibited Naming:** Never append marketing keywords, locations, or superlative titles (e.g. *Dr. Surajit Sinha - Best Criminal Lawyer Birbhum*, *No. 1 Cyber Lawyer Rampurhat*). Appending keywords violates Google's Business Profile representation policy and leads to immediate account suspension.
- Academic honorific `Dr.` and title `Advocate` are permissible when reflecting real-world professional identity.

## 8. First-Party Ownership & Step-by-Step Verification Instructions

### Step 1: Claim Profile with First-Party Google Account
- Dr. Surajit Sinha must sign in using his official Google account (e.g. `sinhasurajit40@gmail.com`).
- Navigate to `https://www.google.com/business/`.
- Search for "Dr. Surajit Sinha" or click "Add your business to Google".

### Step 2: Input Approved Information
- Business Name: `Dr. Surajit Sinha`
- Primary Category: `Lawyer`
- Input confirmed address, service areas, phone number, and official website URL (`https://surajitsinha.com`).

### Step 3: Complete Google Verification
- **Video Verification (Most Common):** Google frequently requests a 1-2 minute continuous smartphone video showing:
  - Outside street signage and building exterior.
  - Opening the chamber door with keys.
  - Interior office, desk, legal reference books, and computer setup.
- **Postcard Verification (Alternative):** Google mails a 5-digit verification PIN to the physical address (delivered in 10-15 business days).

### Step 4: Grant Technical Agency / Manager Access
- Once verified, Dr. Surajit Sinha remains the **Primary Owner**.
- To allow the developer/agency to manage updates, go to **Business Profile Settings → Managers → Add** and invite the technical agency as a **Manager** (never transfer Primary Ownership).
