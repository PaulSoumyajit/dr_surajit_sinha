## 2. Professional Compliance Safeguards

1. **No Fictional Portraiture:** In strict adherence to `docs/04_CONTENT_REQUIREMENTS.md`, no stock photography or AI-generated faces are used to represent Advocate Surojit Sinha. The hero uses pure architectural photography until authentic formal portraiture is supplied by the advocate.

3. **Responsive WebP Optimization:** Automatically transcoded and resized on the fly by Next.js image optimization pipeline.
